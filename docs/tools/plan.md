# Check a launch plan

Paste or upload the plan as a sheet with three columns: the old address (Old URL), the new address
(New URL) and the title (Title). It is read **in this page**: nothing is uploaded or stored. It
reports:

- **An old address with no destination:** after launch it answers 404.
- **Two rows sharing one title:** search results can't tell the pages apart.

The [plan check](/docs/plan-check) does much more on your own computer: duplicate addresses,
missing titles and H1s, slugs, keyword overlap, and, with `--base`, the built site and its 301s.
