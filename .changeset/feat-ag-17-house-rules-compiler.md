New `gaugeline house-rules --rules <file>`: turns a written style guide (headings in sentence case, no em dashes, buttons say Book now, never use the word cheap, phone numbers formatted like 000 000 0000) into a draft `gaugeline.rules.draft.json` for you to review and rename. It lists lines it does not understand, refuses patterns and file paths, and never edits a live file.
