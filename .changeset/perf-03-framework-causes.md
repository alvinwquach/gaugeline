Four new checks for what a framework tends to leave behind: a 3D scene that starts at load with no click or tap (`perf-webgl-autostart`), a first large image that is lazy, late or oversized (`perf-lcp-image`), an animation that makes the browser lay the page out every frame (`perf-animation-reflow`), and a heavy page with no Save-Data or low-end-device fallback (`perf-no-lowend-fallback`).
