With `--renderer playwright`, five new accessibility checks that use what the browser computes: text contrast against its real background (`a11y-contrast`), keyboard focus order (`a11y-focus-order`), control names and unreachable widgets (`a11y-names-roles`), landmarks (`a11y-landmarks`), and animation with no reduced-motion rule (`a11y-reduced-motion`).
