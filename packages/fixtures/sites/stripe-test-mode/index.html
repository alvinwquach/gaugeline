La Olla Pop-Up

An invented pop-up kitchen for Gaugeline's tests: tamales out of a borrowed bakery every Saturday morning, sold by the box and picked up between nine and noon. The kitchen is a rented counter at the back of a bakery on Elm Street, so the menu changes with what the bakery's ovens are doing that week, and the first box of the day is always the one with the red salsa.
