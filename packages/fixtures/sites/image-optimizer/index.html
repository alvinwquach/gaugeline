Cedar and Crumb Bakery

An invented neighbourhood bakery for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business. Its website is built with Next.js and resizes the photos of the loaves through the framework's image address.

We bake sourdough, rye and seeded loaves every morning from six, and croissants, cardamom buns and saturday morning focaccia while the oven is still warm. Pre-order a loaf for the weekend by the Thursday before, collect it at the counter on Cedar Street, and come for coffee while you wait. We are a small team of five bakers and a Labrador called Biscuit who supervises the door, and we are open every day except Monday, when the ovens rest and the starter gets fed.
