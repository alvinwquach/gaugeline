Quillfeather Bakery

Fresh bread, baked at dawn.

An invented bakery for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business or person. The bakery sells sourdough, rye and seeded loaves, with a counter open from six in the morning and orders taken the day before. A seasonal tray of buns joins the shelves every weekend, and the shop closes when the bread runs out.

Quillfeather Bakery, an invented business.
