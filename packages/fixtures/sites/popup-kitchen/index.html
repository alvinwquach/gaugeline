Ember Street Kitchen

Summer 2025 menu: every Thursday through September 2025, 5 to 9 pm.

Ember Street Kitchen is a pop-up that sets up its wood-fired oven in a different courtyard each week. We make flatbreads from a slow-rising dough, top them with whatever the growers down the road brought us that morning, and bake each one in about ninety seconds. Bring a blanket, bring friends, and come hungry. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are always on the board, and the oven never closes before the last order is baked. Follow us for where we are setting up next.
