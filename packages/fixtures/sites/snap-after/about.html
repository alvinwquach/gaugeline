About

Our studio

The studio is a converted dairy at the end of a lane, with four wheels, a slab roller and a long table where the glazing happens. Two potters work here full time and a third joins us at weekends. Every piece is thrown, trimmed and signed by hand, then left to dry slowly on the shelves above the kiln before its first firing.

Visit us

The shop opens Wednesday to Sunday from ten until five. There is parking in the yard and a bench under the walnut tree. Dogs on leads are welcome, and so are muddy boots.
