Fern and Kiln Pottery

Fern and Kiln is an invented pottery studio, made up for the page snapshot checks. We throw stoneware on the wheel, glaze it in small batches and fire it in a gas kiln at the back of the yard. Nothing on this page describes a real business: the words are here so the page reads like one, and so the checks have something to count.
