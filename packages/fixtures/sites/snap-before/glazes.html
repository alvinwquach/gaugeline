Glaze notes

A short article on how we mix the house celadon. We weigh the dry ingredients on a kitchen scale, sieve them twice through a fine mesh and let the bucket settle overnight. The colour depends on the iron in the clay and on how much air the kiln gets near the top of the firing, so no two batches match. Write the recipe down every time and keep a test tile of each batch.
