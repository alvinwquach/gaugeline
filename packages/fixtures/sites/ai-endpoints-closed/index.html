Cedar Row Tea Room

An invented tea room for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business or person. The menu helper on this site answers questions about teas, allergens and opening hours through a route on the site's own server, which talks to a model. Cakes are baked each morning, loose-leaf teas are served by the pot, and the back room can be booked for small gatherings on weekday afternoons and on Sunday mornings.
