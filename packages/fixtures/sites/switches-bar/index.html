The Copper Lantern

An invented neighbourhood bar with a short kitchen menu. Nothing here is a real business, number or address: it exists so Gaugeline's availability checks have a site to read.

The kitchen cooks from five until ten every night, and what is gone is gone: when the cook runs out of something, the bar writes it on the 86 list and the staff stop selling it. The menu and the Tonight page are meant to follow that list, and this fixture is the site that did not follow it.
