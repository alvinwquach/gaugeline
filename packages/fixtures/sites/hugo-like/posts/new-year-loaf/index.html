The New Year loaf

An invented post for Gaugeline's tests. Every January we bake one loaf that is a little bit silly: this year it is a rye and orange loaf with a crust of toasted fennel seed, scored by hand with a razor and left to rest for a full day before it goes in the oven. We make forty of them, and when they are gone they are gone. Order one by Friday and collect it on the first Saturday of the month, between eight and eleven, from the counter at the back of the bakery.

The loaf keeps for four days wrapped in a cloth, and it makes very good toast. Ask at the counter if you would like it sliced, and bring your own bag if you can.
