Corner Pantry

Corner Pantry is an invented neighbourhood shop used only in Gaugeline's tests. It sells bread, preserves and a few tins on a street that does not exist, and its website was built years ago on a theme the owner has never wanted to touch because it still works. Every name and address on this page is made up, and no real business is described here. The shop opens at nine, the bread arrives at eight, and the owner answers questions about orders by phone between the morning rush and lunch.
