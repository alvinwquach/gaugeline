Harbour Lane Tap Room

An invented pop-up bar for Gaugeline's Sanity image checks. We pour six local beers and a short cocktail list from a converted fishing container, Fridays from five until the last ferry. Everything here is made up: the bar, the drinks and every picture address below.

The owner uploads the photos from her phone in Sanity Studio, draws the crop on the menu board and drags the hotspot onto the faces in the team photo. The front end decides what reaches the page.

Book the container for a birthday or a team night: forty people, a tab, and a playlist of your own.
