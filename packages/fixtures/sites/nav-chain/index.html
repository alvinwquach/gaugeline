Marlow Vets

Marlow Vets is an invented veterinary practice, made up for the menu link chain check. We see cats, dogs and rabbits by appointment, run a Saturday nurse clinic for vaccinations and microchipping, and keep two consulting rooms and a small recovery ward. Nothing on this page describes a real business: the words are here so the page reads like one, and so the menu has links to follow.
