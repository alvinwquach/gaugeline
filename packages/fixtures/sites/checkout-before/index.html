Harbour Street Physio is an invented physiotherapy clinic, made up for Gaugeline's script and header baseline checks. We treat sports injuries, back pain and the stiff shoulders that come from a long week at a desk. A first visit lasts forty-five minutes: we ask about your week, test how you move and agree a short plan with exercises you can do at home. Follow-up visits last thirty minutes. Appointments can be booked and paid for on this page. The card form is loaded from our payment provider, so card numbers never touch our own server. The prices on this page are made up, the names are made up, and nothing here describes a real clinic.
