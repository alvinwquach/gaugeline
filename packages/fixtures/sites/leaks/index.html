Bramble Ceramics

An invented pottery studio for Gaugeline's tests: wheel classes on weeknights, open studio on Saturdays, and a small shop of mugs and bowls glazed in the back room. Nothing here is a real business, key or dataset.

Classes run for six weeks and include clay, glazes and two firings. Book a place on the classes page; the shop restocks on the first Saturday of the month.
