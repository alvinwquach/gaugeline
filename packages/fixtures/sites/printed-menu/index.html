Marigold Taco Pop-Up

Fridays and Saturdays on the corner lot, 5pm until 11pm. We cook everything to order on one flat-top and two grills, so the line moves steadily and nobody waits long. The tortillas are pressed in the morning, the salsas are made fresh every afternoon, and the horchata is mixed in small batches so it never sits. Bring a friend, bring a chair, and come hungry. If the weather turns, we move under the awning and keep serving until the last order is up.
