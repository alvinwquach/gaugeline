# printed-menu

An invented taco pop-up, Marigold Taco Pop-Up, for the document checks (F-12). Nothing here is a real
business, item or price.

- `/`: this season's menu in the page's text (Carne Asada Taco $4.00, Elote Cup $6.00, Horchata $5.00)
  and hours of Friday and Saturday 5pm to 11pm in its structured data.
- `/menu.pdf`: last season's printable menu, made by `makePdf` in `packages/testkit/src/pdf.ts`
  (Carne Asada Taco $3.50, Elote Cup $6.00, Horchata $4.00, open Fri-Sat 5pm-10pm).
