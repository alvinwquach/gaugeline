The Lantern Bar

The Lantern Bar is an invented neighbourhood bar, made up for the menu campaign tag checks. We pour a short list of cocktails, keep a handful of small plates warm until late, and host a quiz on Tuesdays and live music on Fridays. The back room seats thirty for private dinners. Nothing on this page describes a real business: the words are here so the page reads like one, and so the checks have a menu to follow.
