The BUCK Tavern

An invented neighbourhood bar for Gaugeline's tests, formerly the Buckhorn. Twelve taps, a short menu of things on toast, and a pool table nobody has ever won on. Nothing here is a real business.

Open Monday to Thursday from 4pm to 1am, Friday and Saturday from 4pm to 2am, and Sunday from noon to 10pm. We are closed on Thanksgiving Day and on Christmas Day.

The BUCK Tavern, 412 Alder Street, Fairmont Heights. Call (217) 555-0142.
