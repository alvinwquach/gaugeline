Bellwether Bike Repair

An invented bike shop for Gaugeline's tests. Book a service online and we email a confirmation, then a note when the bike is ready to collect. Tune-ups, wheel truing, brake bleeds and the odd rescue of a bike that spent the winter in a shed.

The shop sends its booking emails through one service and its newsletter through another, and somebody added an SPF record for each instead of one record for both. Nothing here is a real business or domain.
