Harbour Books

An invented second-hand bookshop for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business, person or address. The site moved off WordPress years ago, and its old download trigger still waits for a link under /wp-content/ that no page has any more.

We buy and sell used fiction, poetry and local history, and hold a reading on the first Friday of every month. Bring a box of books on a weekday morning and we will make you an offer while you browse the shelves upstairs.
