Larkspur Yoga

An invented yoga studio for Gaugeline's tests: morning flow on weekdays, a gentle class on Saturdays and a beginners' course each autumn. Ask the booking helper which class suits you, and it answers from the timetable. Nothing here is a real business, person or key.

Classes are small, with at most twelve people on the mats, and every first class is free. The studio has mats, blocks and blankets to lend, a changing room, and a tea corner where the teachers sit with anyone who wants to talk through the week's classes before they book. Evening classes start at half past six and run for an hour, and the weekend class begins at nine.
