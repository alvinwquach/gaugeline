Tideline Coaching

Tideline Coaching is an invented one-person running and strength coach used only in Gaugeline's tests. Every name, address and file on this page is made up, and no real business is described here. Members get their plan, the class notes and a members guide through the site, and each of those links goes to a download route that is meant to check the purchase before it hands anything over. Sessions are held on the beach path at low tide, in a small studio when the weather turns, and over video for clients who live away.
