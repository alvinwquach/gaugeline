An invented ceramics store for Gaugeline's tests, built on Hydrogen. A speckled stoneware mug, thrown and glazed in the back room on Marrow Lane. Pick up on Saturdays or have it posted. Nothing here is a real business or deployment.

Each mug is thrown on the wheel, trimmed by hand, dipped in a speckled white glaze and fired twice, so no two are the same. It holds about three hundred and fifty millilitres, goes in the dishwasher and the microwave, and ships in a recycled cardboard box with a card from the maker. Local orders are ready within a week; posted orders leave on Mondays and Thursdays.
