Spoke and Chain

Flat tyres, gears and brakes fixed while you wait. Open Tuesday to Saturday, nine until six, in the old tram shed behind the market. Most repairs take under an hour, and we will tell you the price before we touch the bike. Bring the bike, a coffee is on us while you sit by the stand and watch the work.

A basic service covers new cables, a clean drivetrain, adjusted brakes and gears, pumped tyres and a short test ride. Wheel truing, hub rebuilds and bottom brackets are quoted on the day. Kids bikes and cargo bikes are welcome, and so are electric bikes with the battery taken out.
