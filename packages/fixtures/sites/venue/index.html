Copper Kettle Tavern

An invented neighbourhood tavern for Gaugeline's event checks. Trivia every Thursday, karaoke on Saturdays, and a darts league whose nights move around. Nothing here is a real venue, address or telephone number, and the 555-01xx number is reserved for fiction.

The kitchen serves until nine, the quiz starts once the tables are full, and the karaoke list opens at half past eight. Teams of up to six for trivia; sign up at the bar.
