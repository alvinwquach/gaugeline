Thimble Soup Kitchen

An invented lunch counter for Gaugeline's tests. Three soups a day, bread from the bakery next door, and orders taken online for pickup at noon. Nothing here is a real business or database.

Monday is split pea with ham, Wednesday a white bean and kale, Friday a red lentil with lemon. Every bowl comes with a heel of sourdough and a pat of salted butter, and refills are half price until the pot runs dry, which on a cold day is usually before one.

The menu and the orders live in an invented Supabase project, gaugelinefixture, whose answers are recorded in packages/testkit/cassettes/supabase-access.json.
