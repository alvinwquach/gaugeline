Brambleworth Hardware

An invented hardware shop for Gaugeline's fixture tests. Screws by the gram, paint mixed while you wait, and a garden centre round the back that opens from March to October. Ask at the counter for key cutting, knife sharpening and the loan of a ladder for the weekend.

Write to the counter, call us, or open the chat. Staff can sign in at the staff page; the stock search is at stock search, and last year's catalogue is at the old catalogue. We also sell at the Saturday market.
