Marrow & Rye Bakery

An invented bakery for Gaugeline's Sanity image checks. We bake sourdough, rye and seeded loaves every morning from five, laminated pastry from seven, and a small tray of Saturday buns that is gone by ten. Everything here is made up: the shop, the loaves and every picture address below.

The shop owner photographs each new loaf on her phone and uploads it in Sanity Studio. Pictures come from the Sanity image network, and the site decides how each one is asked for.

Order ahead for the Saturday bread club: six loaves a month, collected from the counter, with a slice of whatever we are testing that week.
