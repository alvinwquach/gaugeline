Fernlight Cafe

An invented cafe for Gaugeline's tests: coffee roasted next door, cakes baked at six, and a back room that seats twelve. Nothing here is a real business or deployment.

The freelancer who built this site left a Netlify deploy preview in the sitemap while checking the menu page with the owner.

Opening hours are seven to four on weekdays and eight to two at the weekend. We bake the lemon cake, the cheese scones and the seeded loaves ourselves, and we roast the filter coffee in the unit next door every Tuesday and Friday. Bring a book, bring a dog, bring the whole family: the back room seats twelve and can be booked for birthdays, book clubs and the occasional small wedding breakfast.
