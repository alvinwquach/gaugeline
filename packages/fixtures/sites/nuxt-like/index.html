Marrow & Rye Bakery

An invented neighbourhood bakery for Gaugeline's tests, built with Nuxt. Sourdough baked at four in the morning, cardamom buns on Saturdays, and a counter that sells out by noon. Nothing here is a real business or deployment.

Order a loaf for the weekend or ask about a wedding cake. The site was copied from a development build: it still loads the dev server client, its canonical address points at a preview, and its shop settings carry fields that should have stayed on the server.
