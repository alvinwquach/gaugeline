Tideline Coaching

Tideline Coaching is an invented one-person running and strength coach used only in Gaugeline's tests. Every name, price and address on this page is made up, and no real business is described here. Sessions are held on the beach path at low tide, in a small studio when the weather turns, and over video for clients who live away. The coach writes each programme by hand for the person it is for, checks in by message every week, and rewrites the plan when a hamstring, a night shift or a new baby changes what the week can hold.
