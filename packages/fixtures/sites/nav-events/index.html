Ridgeback Roasters

An invented coffee roastery for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business, person or address. The menu was rebuilt last month: every link is meant to send a menu_click event with its text, its destination and the menu it sits under, so the new menu can be compared with the old one.

We roast small batches every Tuesday and Friday, sell beans by the bag at the counter and online, and run a tasting on the last Saturday of each month. Bring your own cup for a discount on any drink.
