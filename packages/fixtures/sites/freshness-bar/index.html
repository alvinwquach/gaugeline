Juniper Tap House

An invented neighbourhood bar with a weekly specials board and a Thursday trivia night. Nothing here is a real business, number or address: it exists so Gaugeline's data freshness checks have a site to read.

The kitchen is open from noon until ten, and the bar stays open later on weekends. On Mondays the owner writes the week's specials on the board by the door and posts the same list on the Specials page, so that regulars can plan their week. The What's on page lists the trivia nights and the live music that are still to come, and each one is meant to come off the list the day after it happens.
