Marigold Tamales

An invented weekend tamal pop-up on Copper Street. Pork, chicken and rajas tamales steamed every Friday and Saturday evening at the side window, from five until the last tamal is gone. Nothing here is a real business, number or payment handle: it exists so Gaugeline's switch checks have a site to read.

We make one hundred dishes a night, no more, so that every tamal is hot when it is handed over. Pre-orders open when the week's masa is ready and close when the cap is reached; on the days they are closed, you order at the window and we take cash or card until the dishes run out.
