Thimble & Thread

An invented haberdashery for Gaugeline's image checks. Buttons by the jar, ribbons by the metre, thread in four hundred colours and a sewing class every Thursday evening. Every picture on this page is made up and none of the files exist outside the fixture.

The shop opens at nine on weekdays and ten on Saturdays. Bring a scrap of the fabric you are matching and we will find the closest thread, button or zip from the drawers behind the counter.
