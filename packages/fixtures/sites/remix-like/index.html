Marrow Lane Ceramics

An invented ceramics shop for Gaugeline's tests, built with React Router 7. Speckled mugs, pour-over sets and a small run of dinner plates, thrown and glazed in the back room on Marrow Lane. Pick up on Saturdays or ask for a quote on a restaurant set. Nothing here is a real business or deployment.
