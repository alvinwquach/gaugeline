Rye and Ember is an invented neighbourhood bakery, made up for the navigation checks. We bake sourdough, rye and seeded loaves every morning before six, laminate our pastries by hand, and cater small events across the city. The shop opens at seven on weekdays and eight at weekends, and closes when the bread runs out. Nothing on this page describes a real business: the words are here so the page reads like one.
