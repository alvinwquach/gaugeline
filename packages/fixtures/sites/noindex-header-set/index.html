Dockside Coffee

An invented pop-up cafe for Gaugeline's tests. Nothing here is a real business. The site is hidden from search until the opening day: the pages say noindex in their metadata, and the editor studio and the API sit at addresses that no page metadata reaches. We will serve single-origin coffee, pastries baked each morning and a short lunch menu from a converted harbour container, from the first Saturday of December. Until then the whole site stays out of search, and the team checks that nothing leaks before the doors open to the public.
