Larkspur Tea Room

An invented tea room for fixture tests. Loose-leaf teas, scones baked each morning, and a seasonal menu that changes with the garden. The journal feed lists recent posts; the menu feed lists what changed this week; the events feed has moved and no longer answers.

The tea room opens at eight with a pot of the house breakfast blend, and closes at four after the last round of afternoon tea. Tables by the window look over the herb beds, where the mint and lemon verbena for the tisanes are picked each day. Groups of six or more can book the long table in the back room.
