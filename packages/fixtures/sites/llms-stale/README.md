# llms-stale

An invented bakery on `hollyhock.test` whose llms.txt has gone stale:

- The phone number is old: llms.txt says (503) 555-0119, while the structured data says +1 503-555-0142. Both are in the 555-01xx range reserved for fiction.
- Saturday closes at 1pm in llms.txt and at 2pm in the structured data.
- `/catering` is a 404, and `/order` redirects off-site to `orders.test`.
