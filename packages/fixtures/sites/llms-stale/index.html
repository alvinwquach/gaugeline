Hollyhock Bakery

An invented business for Gaugeline's fixture tests. Fresh bread from six in the morning, pastries until they run out, and coffee roasted a few streets away. The counter takes orders for celebration cakes with two days' notice, and the back room seats twelve for small gatherings. Nothing here is a real business, address or telephone number.

Opening hours, the telephone number and the street address are published in the page's structured data, so search engines and assistants can quote them without guessing.
