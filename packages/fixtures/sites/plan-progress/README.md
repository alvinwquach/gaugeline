# plan-progress

An invented pottery studio's 10-row launch plan (`plan.csv`, on `kilnlane.example`) and its pages,
all built and matching, for the plan progress between runs (G17b, `--progress`). Nothing here is a
real business.

The three week folders serve these pages with some of them not built yet, as a preview fills in:

- `plan-progress-week1`: 6 of 10 built (`/classes/handbuilding`, `/shop/mugs`, `/visit` and
  `/gift-cards` are 404).
- `plan-progress-week2`: 9 of 10 built (only `/gift-cards` is 404): 3 fixed, 0 new.
- `plan-progress-week3`: week 2 with the `/shop` title changed to "Shop all | Kiln Lane": 1 new.
