An invented neighbourhood bakery: sourdough, morning buns and Saturday focaccia, baked before dawn. The shop's freelancer shipped the site with its build's extras still on. Nothing here is a real business.

Order a loaf for Saturday pickup, or ask about a standing weekly order for your cafe. Opening hours are 7 to 2 on weekdays and 8 to 1 on weekends, and the oven is never off before noon.
