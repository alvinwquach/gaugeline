The walk-in hour moves to half past seven

From the first of the month the nurse-led walk-in hour starts at half past seven instead of eight, so people can be seen before work. The clinic is an invented one that exists only in Gaugeline's tests, and nothing on this page describes a real business or a real person. Patients who cannot make that time can still use the evening session on Thursdays, which is unchanged. The front desk will move any booking that now falls in a different slot, and it will call the people who are affected before the change takes place, so nobody has to do anything.
