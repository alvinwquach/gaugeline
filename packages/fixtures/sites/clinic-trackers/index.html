Meadowlark Family Clinic

Meadowlark Family Clinic is an invented clinic used only in Gaugeline's tests. It has four rooms, a nurse-led walk-in hour every morning and a front desk that answers the phone from eight until five. Every name and address on this page is made up, and no real business is described here. New patients can use the intake page before a first visit, and everyone else can read the clinic news or look at how the nurse practitioner looks after people with asthma. The front desk will answer any other question by phone.
