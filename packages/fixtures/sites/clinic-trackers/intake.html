New patient intake

Tell us about yourself before your first visit, so the nurse has what she needs. The form takes your name, your date of birth and the reason you are coming in, and it goes to the clinic's intake service, which is run by another company. This page belongs to an invented clinic that exists only in Gaugeline's tests, and nothing you could type here goes anywhere. If you would rather not use the form, the front desk takes the same details by phone from eight until five on any weekday, and a nurse will call you back within a working day.
