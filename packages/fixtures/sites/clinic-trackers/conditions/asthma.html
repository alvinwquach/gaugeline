Asthma care

The clinic's asthma service is run by an invented nurse practitioner who reviews each patient once a year and gives a written plan for bad days. This page describes a service of a clinic that exists only in Gaugeline's tests. The plan covers what to do on a good day, what to do when a cough starts to wake you at night, and which signs mean the same day appointment is needed. The nurse practitioner reviews the plan with the patient each autumn, before the colder months begin, and the clinic keeps a spare reliever for anyone who forgets theirs.
